IMPORTANT — THE COMPANY IS NOT AN AIR CARRIER. It does not hold an air carrier or commercial operator certificate, does not hold out to the public or to any segment of the public the provision of air transportation for compensation or hire, and does not sell, broker or arrange flights as a commercial air service. Where a client requires commercial air transportation, the operator of that flight must be a certificated air carrier and the client contracts with that operator directly. OPERATIONAL CONTROL: operational control means the exercise of authority over initiating, conducting or terminating a flight. WHICH PARTY HOLDS OPERATIONAL CONTROL OF A FLIGHT DETERMINES WHICH OPERATING RULES APPLY, and the company records that allocation in writing before services are provided in relation to any flight. A management arrangement under which the manager exercises operational control while a flight is held out for compensation constitutes an unauthorized commercial operation, commonly described as illegal charter, irrespective of the parties' intentions or of how the arrangement is labelled. THE COMPANY DECLINES ANY ARRANGEMENT THAT WOULD HAVE THAT EFFECT. Clients and aircraft owners are advised that a flight conducted outside the operating rules under which it was presented may fall outside the scope of the applicable insurance policy, with the consequence that a claim may be declined, and that enforcement exposure in such cases extends to the owner as well as to any operator. LEASING: the distinction between a lease of an aircraft without crew and an arrangement that includes crew is material, an arrangement providing both aircraft and crew generally constituting air transportation; the company does not structure or participate in arrangements designed to present commercial air transportation as private operation. FLIGHT INSTRUCTION AND MAINTENANCE ARE NOT PROVIDED by the company: flight instruction requires appropriately certificated instructors operating under the applicable training rules, and maintenance, preventive maintenance, alterations and inspections require an appropriately certificated repair station or certificated mechanic. Any such work is referred to a certificated provider and is not performed by the company; certificates held by the company, if any, are limited to those stated at [CERTIFICATES HELD, IF ANY]. AIRWORTHINESS AND PILOT CURRENCY remain the responsibility of the aircraft owner and of the pilot in command respectively; the company does not certify airworthiness and does not authorize any flight. NO SCHEDULE, AVAILABILITY OR PERFORMANCE IS GUARANTEED: weather, air traffic control, airport conditions, maintenance findings and crew availability all affect a flight, and the pilot in command's authority to refuse or terminate a flight is absolute and is not overridden by any commercial arrangement.
