What we do, in detail

Four services, all starting from who holds operational control.

Program 01 · Owner keeps control

Aircraft management

Management work is administrative, logistical and financial: coordinating vendors, tracking budgets, keeping records in order. What it is not is authority to initiate, conduct or terminate flights, unless the arrangement says so and the applicable rules are met. We write the allocation down because a vague management agreement is how owners end up in an operation they never agreed to.

  • Per arrangement
  • Aircraft owners
  • Written allocation
  • Not an air carrier

Program 02 · Where the line sits

Arrangement review

Owners are routinely offered arrangements that look efficient and sit on the wrong side of the line: an aircraft plus crew presented as a lease, cost sharing that functions as a fare, a management company exercising control over flights held out for money. We will tell you when something has that shape, and we decline to participate in it.

  • Per review
  • Owners and operators
  • Written findings
  • Referral if needed

Program 03 · Certificated providers

Vendor coordination

We coordinate; we do not perform maintenance. Maintenance, alterations and inspections require a certificated repair station or a certificated mechanic, and that is not a boundary anyone should blur on an aircraft. We track what was done, when, by whom and under what authorization, because the records are the aircraft's value as much as its condition.

  • Aircraft owners
  • Certificated vendors
  • Records kept

Program 04 · Read before you fly

Insurance scope support

Aviation policies are specific about use, pilot qualifications and currency, and a flight outside those terms may simply not be covered. Owners discover this after an incident. We read the policy against how the aircraft is actually used and flag the gaps. We are not brokers or advisers on insurance products; we point at the mismatch and send you to your broker.

  • Per review
  • Aircraft owners
  • Policy provided
  • Not insurance advice
